# OCR accuracy — measured results

Generated by `npm run ocr:fixtures && npm run ocr:eval` on 2026-07-20.
Engine: tesseract.js 7.0.0, `tessdata_fast` @ `8741641`, OEM 1 (LSTM), PSM AUTO,
languages `ara+eng`, self-hosted from `/assets/ocr`.

**These numbers are the raw output of the harness, not a target.** Reproduce
them with the two commands above; the corpus regenerates deterministically.

## Corpus

30 fixtures: 5 fabricated documents × 6 capture conditions.

De-identified by construction. Drug names are public INN generics; every
supplier, invoice number, batch code, price and warehouse name is invented. No
production, patient or supplier document is in this repository, and none may be
added.

| Document | Language | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| `en-clean-amoxicillin` | English | Simple delivery note |
| `en-clean-paracetamol` | English | Goods received note, ratio concentration |
| `ar-clean-amoxicillin` | Arabic | RTL, Arabic-Indic digits |
| `bilingual-ceftriaxone` | Arabic + English | Dual-labelled rows |
| `en-dense-invoice` | English | Dense, more fields, mfg + expiry |

Conditions: `scan` (clean), `photo` (handheld softness), `rotated` (3.5°),
`blurred` (out of focus), `glare` (blown highlights), `dim` (poor light).

## Field-level results

Scored only on fields the ground truth declares for that document.

| Field | Correct | **Wrong** | Missed | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| scientificName | 19 | 1 | 4 | 79.2% |
| batchNumber | 10 | 3 | 17 | 33.3% |
| expiryDate | 13 | 0 | 17 | 43.3% |
| quantity | 13 | 0 | 17 | 43.3% |
| nationalCode | 13 | 0 | 11 | 54.2% |
| unitPrice | 9 | 0 | 3 | 75.0% |

**Wrong is the column that matters.** A missed field is typed by the operator;
a wrong field may be waved through. Expiry, quantity, national code and price
produced ZERO wrong values across the corpus — they either read correctly or
not at all. Batch number is the weak field, with 3 wrong readings.

Blur collapses recognition almost completely, which is precisely why the quality
gate treats blur as a hard blocker and asks for a retake before OCR runs. The
rotated result is measured WITHOUT deskew applied; the pipeline can deskew when
the angle is measurable, and that path is not represented in these numbers.

## Two defects this harness found

1. **`scientificName` was 0%.** Extraction was label-only, but real delivery
   notes print the drug as a headline ("Amoxicillin 500 mg Capsules"), not
   behind a "Generic Name:" label. Adding a headline candidate took it to 79.2%.
2. **A garbled label leaked in as a value.** On a rotated scan, "Batch Number
   PC2291" read as "NUM8ER", which passed the batch shape check (alphanumeric,
   contains a digit) and was offered as the batch number. Candidates that fold
   onto a known label word are now rejected.

Both are pinned by unit tests.

## Honest limitations

- **Synthetic corpus.** These are rendered documents with programmatic
  degradation, not photographs of real packaging. Real captures bring
  perspective, curved surfaces, foil glare, mixed fonts and print wear that
  this corpus does not reproduce. Expect real-world accuracy to be **lower**.
- **Overfitting risk.** Both fixes above were derived from this corpus. They are
  principled rather than fixture-specific, but they have not been validated
  against independent documents.
- **Arabic is under-measured.** One Arabic and one bilingual document is not
  enough to characterise Arabic accuracy. The Arabic fixture also omits a
  scientific-name ground truth, so Arabic name extraction is untested.
- **No deskew in the measurement path.** The rotated numbers are a floor.
- **Tier-1 catalog matching is inert.** `central_items` has no national-code
  column, so code-based matching cannot fire against the real catalog
  regardless of how well OCR reads the code.

## Verdict

**This is not production-ready OCR, and must not be described as such.** It is a
measured, reviewable assist whose every output passes through explicit human
confirmation before any stock is written. The accuracy above is adequate for
"pre-fill a form the operator checks" and is NOT adequate for anything
automatic.

Reaching a production claim needs a representative de-identified corpus of real
photographed packaging and invoices, collected with the owner's consent, plus a
re-run of this harness against it.
